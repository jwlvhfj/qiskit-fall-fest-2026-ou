01 / About Us

Who we are and what we do.

We are a team of students passionate about exploring quantum information and computation. Our goal is to build a community of like-minded learners across the University of Oklahoma and its surrounding areas who are excited to join us on this journey into the world of quantum technologies.

02 / Program

Learn it. Build it. Explore it.

Whether you are completely new to quantum computing or already exploring quantum technologies, Fall Fest is designed to help you learn, experiment, collaborate, and discover new ideas.

01

Learn

Explore the foundations of quantum information, computation, qubits, gates, measurement, and quantum circuits.

02

Gain hands-on experience using Qiskit, quantum circuits, simulators, and modern quantum computing tools.

03

Collaborate

Meet students with similar interests, work through challenges together, and help grow a quantum community at OU.

05 / Educational Tools

Keep exploring.

Quantum computing is a rapidly developing field, and there is always more to explore. These resources can help you learn quantum concepts, practice with Qiskit, build circuits, and continue developing your understanding beyond Fall Fest.

You do not need to master everything before joining us. These tools are here for anyone who wants to learn at their own pace and explore quantum computing further.

RESOURCE 01 ↗

IBM Quantum Learning

Courses, modules, tutorials, and guided learning materials covering quantum information, algorithms, and Qiskit.

RESOURCE 02 ↗

Qiskit Guides

Practical documentation for building, simulating, executing, and analyzing quantum circuits with Qiskit.

RESOURCE 03 ↗

IBM Quantum Platform

Explore quantum computing tools, execute circuits, and experiment with quantum workloads and systems.

RESOURCE 04 ↗

Qiskit on GitHub

Explore Qiskit's open-source code, releases, development, examples, and technical resources.

RESOURCE 05 ↗

Quantum Computing for the Very Curious

An approachable introduction designed to develop intuition about quantum computing concepts interactively.

RESOURCE 06 ↗

Qiskit

Explore Qiskit's ecosystem and tools for developing and experimenting with quantum computation.
